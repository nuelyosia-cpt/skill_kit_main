## Set
Xicheng: a rammed-earth and brick town wall running along z, with the gate at x=0. The gate tower is a red-pillared pavilion with a grey tiled roof and upturned eaves. The plain is outside (+x); the town courtyard is inside (−x). The Wei army is in blocks on the plain with red/black banners. A map table sits in a side courtyard. A distant mountain pass stands off to the north (Ma Su). Night light is used for the storyteller twist.
