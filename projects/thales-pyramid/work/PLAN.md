# The Shadow That Measured a Pyramid: shot plan
Voice: user recording, 62.93 s → TOTAL 1888 frames @30 fps. Captions band y≈1180-1420: faces and key objects stay above y≈1150.

## Set
- **Ground:** a desert of sand with low dunes. The Great Pyramid sits at the origin at true scale (base 230 m, height 146 m), with its faces aligned to the x/z axes.
- **Sun:** comes from −x (az 180), so shadows fall toward +x. Elevation is animated: it rises from 22° to exactly 45° on "tall" (f1035), then holds. At 45° every shadow equals its object's height: the pyramid's shadow tip lands 146 m from its centre (x = 146), 31 m beyond the base edge.
- **Thales** stands near the shadow tip (x≈150). In the shadow shots his card has a fixed yaw facing the sun, so its cast shadow is a full silhouette, and the camera looks from a 3/4 angle.
- **Overlay lines** (gold, depth-test off) draw the similar triangles: his height and shadow, then the pyramid's height (an x-ray line through the stone) and its shadow.
- **Props:** a ladder (3D) leaning on the east face, a rope, a tape-measure prop, a map table for the travel shot, a palm grove and an obelisk for the "today" shot.
