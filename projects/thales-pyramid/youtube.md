You can't climb it. You can't drop a rope from the top. So how do you measure the tallest building in the world? 🔺☀️

Around 600 BC, the Greek thinker Thales of Miletus visited Egypt. The story goes that he waited for the one moment in the day when his shadow was exactly as long as he was tall, and then measured the Great Pyramid's shadow. Same moment, same sun, same answer.

📜 Fact check: this is an ancient anecdote. It was written down centuries later by Diogenes Laertius (quoting Hieronymus), and Plutarch tells a slightly different version with a stick. Comparing shadows and triangles really does work, and it's still used to measure trees, buildings and mountains.
