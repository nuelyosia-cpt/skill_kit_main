## Set (one island, built from primitives)
- **Cave:** a rock dome (radius 15 m) open toward +z through a tall mouth. Inside: cheese racks, a sheep pen, a fire pit with a flame card, a straw pile (where the stake hides), a huge wooden wine bowl.
- **Boulder:** a 4.6 m rock that rolls across the mouth (0..1 progress), and aside at dawn.
- **Outside:** grassy slope, rocky hills, a beach and the sea (+z), the Greek ship at the shore, neighbour Cyclopes on two far hilltops at night.
- **Cast (our own designs):** Odysseus (red cloak, brown beard, bronze headband), 3 sailor variants, Polyphemus (card scaled ×3.6 ≈ 8 m: one huge eye, shaggy hair, sheepskin), 2 neighbour Cyclopes, sheep (side-view cards, some with a sailor clinging underneath).
- **Light:** fire (cave interior, warm), night (blue), dawn (golden), storm (end).
