## Pinned comment
Fun fact: in Greek, the fake name "Outis" (Nobody) sounds like "mētis", the word for cunning. Homer was making a pun: Odysseus fooled them with his name and his cleverness at the same time. And his "mistake"? Safe on the ship, he shouted his real name back at the giant, and Polyphemus prayed to his father Poseidon for revenge.
