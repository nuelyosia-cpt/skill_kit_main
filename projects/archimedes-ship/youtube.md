## Pinned comment
The famous line "Give me a place to stand and I will move the Earth" isn't in Plutarch's ship story. It was recorded by later writers such as Pappus of Alexandria, about 500 years after Archimedes. Plutarch's version: he boasted that if there were another world, he could go to it and move this one.
